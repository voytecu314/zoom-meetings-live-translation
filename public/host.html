CONNECTING

1. Create a session

2. Capture Zoom tab audio

  1. Open Zoom in a browser tab (or the PWA tab that plays meeting audio).
  2. Click — a browser dialog appears.
  3. In the dialog choose the Zoom tab, and make sure “Also share tab audio” is checked.
IDLE
Browsers can't feed captured tab audio into their speech recognizer — enabling this transcribes your microphone instead. Use it only if you speak into the mic yourself; otherwise use the mock input below or wait for the server-side transcription provider.
